How it works

Simple for parents, effective for children

Four easy steps from your first message to steady progress, with you informed at every stage.

Getting started

Four steps to steady progress

  1. Book a free trial

    Tell us your child's grade, subject and the times that suit you. It takes two minutes on WhatsApp.

  2. Meet your tutor

    A free 30-minute trial lesson online. The tutor finds out what your child knows and where they need help.

  3. Get a learning plan

    After the trial you receive a short written plan with goals, a weekly schedule and the price.

  4. Learn every week

    Regular one-to-one lessons at the same time each week, with notes after every lesson and a monthly progress report.

What your child needs

  • A laptop or tablet with a camera and microphone
  • A stable internet connection
  • A quiet place to learn, ideally where you can see them
  • Their school books or homework, if they want help with it
