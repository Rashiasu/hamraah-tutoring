How it works

From free trial to steady progress

  1. Book a free trial

    Tell us your child's grade, subject and the times that suit you. It takes two minutes on WhatsApp.

  2. Meet your tutor

    A free 30-minute trial lesson online. The tutor finds out what your child knows and where they need help.

  3. Get a learning plan

    After the trial you receive a short written plan with goals, a weekly schedule and the price.

  4. Learn every week

    Regular one-to-one lessons at the same time each week, with notes after every lesson and a monthly progress report.
