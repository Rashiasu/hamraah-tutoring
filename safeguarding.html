Safeguarding

Your child's safety comes first

Online lessons should be as safe as a classroom. Here is exactly how we protect every child we teach.

Our approach

Four layers of protection

Parents in control

All bookings, messages and scheduling go through you, the parent. Tutors never contact your child privately.

Supervised online lessons

Lessons run on Zoom or Google Meet links we create. You are always welcome to sit in.

Recorded for safety

With your consent, lessons are recorded and kept for 30 days, so any concern can be reviewed, then deleted.

Minimal data

We only ask for your child's first name, grade and school curriculum. We never share or sell family data.

Tutor vetting

Every tutor is checked before they teach

Identity check

We verify every tutor's national ID (CNIC) and meet them on camera.

Police and reference checks

Every tutor provides a police character certificate and two professional references.

Qualifications and subject test

We check degrees and certificates, and tutors pass a subject knowledge test.

Demo lesson and training

Tutors teach a demo lesson we observe, and complete our child safeguarding training.

Rules every tutor follows

  • Teach only on lesson links created by Hamraah
  • Never contact a child by phone, text, email or social media
  • Communicate only with the parent or through our coordinator
  • Keep cameras on, in a quiet, professional setting
  • Never ask a child for personal information or photos
  • Report any concern about a child's welfare to us the same day

What we ask of parents

  • Be at home during lessons, and sit in whenever you wish
  • Have your child learn in a shared space, not a closed bedroom
  • Join or open the lesson link from your own account
  • Tell us straight away if anything worries you

Any parent, child or tutor can raise a concern at any time. We take every report seriously, act on it within 24 hours, and remove any tutor who breaks these rules.

Privacy

Recordings and data

Lesson recordings

With your consent, lessons are recorded so they can be reviewed if a question or concern ever comes up. Recordings are stored privately, are never shared, and are deleted after 30 days. You can opt out of recording at any time.

Your family's data

We ask only for what we need: your name and contact details, and your child's first name, grade and curriculum. We never sell or share family data, and we delete it on request when you stop lessons.

Report a concern

Worried about something?

Contact us straight away by WhatsApp on +92 335 4471262 or by email with the subject "Safeguarding". If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first (911 in the USA, 999 in the UK, 112 in Europe).
